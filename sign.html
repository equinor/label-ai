Add C2PA Content Credentials to an image, signed in your browser, and download the result.

This adds Content Credentials (a C2PA manifest) to your image. Drop in an image, add an optional attribution statement, then Sign and download image signs it in your browser and downloads the result in one step — the attribution statement is embedded in the manifest. Signing uses a self-signed certificate, so the credentials are cryptographically valid and viewable, but verifiers (e.g. contentcredentials.org) will show the signer as an unknown / untrusted source. JPEG and PNG keep their format; other formats are converted to PNG.
