Add C2PA Content Credentials to an image, signed in your browser, and download the result.

This adds Content Credentials (a C2PA manifest) to your image. When you load an image, any existing Content Credentials are checked against the C2PA trust list and shown, with the full details. Choose a digital source type, add an optional attribution statement, then Sign and download image signs it in your browser and downloads the result in one step — the attribution statement is embedded in the manifest. If the image already has Content Credentials, they are preserved as a parent in the new manifest (a provenance chain) rather than replaced. Signing uses a self-signed certificate, so the credentials are cryptographically valid and viewable, but verifiers (e.g. contentcredentials.org) will show the signer as an unknown / untrusted source. JPEG and PNG keep their format; other formats are converted to PNG.

Note that there are criticisms of C2PA, and it is not a perfect solution for reliable attribution. See this article for a thorough look at the issues.
