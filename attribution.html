Fill out the form to get started.
The form builds a machine- and human-readable attribution statement following IBM's AI Attribution 1.0 specification. As you fill out the form, the attribution statements update; the full and abbreviated statements link to aiattribution.github.io, where they can be decoded. The expanded statement is a plain-language summary, and the Explanation block is a key to the abbreviations. Use the Copy buttons to copy a statement as plain text — the link is preserved when you paste into applications that understand links.
